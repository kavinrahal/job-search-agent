# Email Category Taxonomy

## Job-Search Related Categories

### application_confirmation
Automated or manual confirmation that a job application was received.
- "Thank you for applying to [Role] at [Company]"
- "Your application has been received"
- "We've got your application and will be in touch"

### rejection
A response indicating the application was unsuccessful at any stage.
- "We've decided to move forward with other candidates"
- "Unfortunately, we won't be proceeding with your application"
- Post-interview rejection at any stage

### interview_invitation
An invitation to participate in an interview at any stage: phone screen, technical assessment, panel, final round.
- "We'd like to invite you to a phone screen"
- "You've been selected for our technical interview"
- "We'd like to schedule a video call to discuss your application"

### recruiter_outreach
Unsolicited contact from a recruiter, headhunter, or hiring manager about a potential opportunity.
- "I came across your profile and think you'd be a great fit"
- "We have an opportunity at [Company] that matches your background"
- LinkedIn InMail forwarded as email

### scheduling_request
A request to find or confirm a time for an upcoming call or interview.
- "Please use this link to book a time that works for you"
- "What times are you available this week?"
- Calendly, Greenhouse, or other scheduling tool invitations

### offer
A formal or informal job offer, or notification that an offer is being prepared.
- "We're pleased to offer you the position of..."
- "We'd like to extend an offer"
- Offer letter or contract attached

### follow_up_needed
An email that requires the candidate to take some action to progress.
- Take-home coding challenge or assessment link
- Request for references or referee details
- Request for additional documents (ID, portfolio, work samples)
- Any email where a reply or action is clearly expected to keep the process moving

### job_alert
An automated digest or notification from a job board listing new job postings that match a saved search. The email is from the platform itself (Seek, LinkedIn, Indeed, etc.), not from a company or recruiter.
- "New jobs matching your search: Software Engineer in Melbourne"
- "5 new jobs for your saved search"
- Seek, LinkedIn, Indeed, or Jora job alert digest emails

## Not Job-Search Related

### not_relevant
Anything clearly unrelated to employment: newsletters, receipts, bank statements, personal correspondence, social notifications, marketing emails.
